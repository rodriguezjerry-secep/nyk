Posting tips

  • Fill in only what you’re adding. Blank questions don’t erase anything.
  • Staffing and announcements from everyone are combined.
  • One item per line. Paste links as-is.
  • Sign in with your secep.net account or your post won’t show.

Add NYK Post to your iPhone

  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen, then Add.

Add NYK Post to your phone
